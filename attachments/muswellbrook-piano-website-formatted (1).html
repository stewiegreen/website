Good to know

Questions parents often ask.

Does my child need previous piano experience?

No. Lessons are specifically designed to welcome complete beginners.

What should we have at home?

A piano or good-quality keyboard is essential for practice. Ideally it should have 88 weighted, velocity-sensitive keys; 61 velocity-sensitive keys is the minimum recommended.

How much should students practise?

Aim for around 10–15 minutes each day. Younger children may benefit from a parent helping them establish a regular practice routine.

What books will my child use?

Beginners commonly start with Faber Piano Adventures or Alfred's All-in-One Course. Additional theory and repertoire books are introduced as students progress.

What happens if we miss a lesson?

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice if possible. A make-up lesson may be arranged when availability permits, but a replacement time cannot be guaranteed.
